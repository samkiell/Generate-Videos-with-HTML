Kiv wordmark
============

The top banner renders the Kiv wordmark via CSS (#top-banner in index.html):
a small lime tile (#top-banner-mark) + "Kiv" in Syne Bold, white
(#top-banner-wordmark). No asset file required.

This is the brand wordmark — DO NOT replace the text "Kiv". Unlike the
brand-neutral classic template (which ships a "YOUR BRAND" placeholder you must
swap), Kiv's wordmark is the real, final brand mark and stays as-is on every Kiv
video.

Casing: "Kiv" (capital K, lowercase iv) — NOT "KIV" all-caps, NOT "kiv"
all-lowercase. The mixed-case form is the brand.

To use an SVG logo instead of the CSS wordmark:
  1. Drop your SVG into this assets/ folder (e.g. kiv-logo.svg).
  2. Replace #top-banner-content's children with a single
     <img id="top-banner-logo" src="assets/kiv-logo.svg" alt="Kiv" /> sized to
     ~52px height. Keep it left-aligned at top:72px / left:72px.
  3. Re-run `npx hyperframes lint videos/<slug>`.
